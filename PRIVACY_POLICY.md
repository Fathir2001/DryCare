# DryCare Privacy Policy (publication draft)

Last updated: 22 October 2026

DryCare is a general skincare and wellness guide. It does not require an account.

## Information stored on your device

When you use DryCare, the app stores the name and age you enter, answers to the skincare questionnaire, the resulting guide score, water entries, routine checklist progress, streak information, and theme preference. This information is stored in the app's local Hive database. DryCare uses it to show your guide and retain progress between sessions. The local database is not separately encrypted, so your device's security controls protect it. Android backup is disabled for the app.

## Network use and sharing

DryCare has no developer backend, account service, advertising, or analytics. The app does not intentionally send your entries to the developer or share them with advertisers. The app uses the Google Fonts package; font loading behavior must be verified in the final release before a claim of fully offline operation is published. Device and platform services outside DryCare's control may process app installation or diagnostic information under their own policies.

## Retention and deletion

Entries remain on the device until you choose Settings > Reset All Data or uninstall the app. Reset All Data clears the stored profile, answers, results, daily tracking, streak, and preferences. The reset cannot remove copies that may exist outside the app's local storage.

## Children

The intended audience and minimum age for publication have not yet been finalized. The in-app age entry currently accepts ages from 5 to 120; this must be aligned with the final Play target-audience declaration before release.

## Health information

The questionnaire and suggestions are for general skincare and wellness information. They are not a diagnosis, clinical severity assessment, or treatment plan. Consult a qualified professional for persistent, worsening, painful, or otherwise concerning symptoms.
