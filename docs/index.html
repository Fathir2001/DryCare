Privacy Policy

How DryCare handles the information you enter while using its skincare and wellness guide.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

DryCare does not require an account. The personal and skincare information you enter is stored on your device to provide your guide and retain your progress. The current Android release has no Internet permission and no developer server, advertising, or analytics integration.

Information stored on your device

DryCare stores the following in its local Hive database when you use the corresponding features:

  • The name and age you enter.
  • Your skincare questionnaire answers, calculated guide score, and related result.
  • Daily water entries, morning and nighttime routine checklist progress, streak count, and activity dates.
  • Onboarding and questionnaire completion status, and your light or dark theme preference.

The app does not request your email address, phone number, precise location, photos, or payment information.

How the information is used

DryCare uses your entries on your device to display your questionnaire result, skincare suggestions, daily tracking, routine progress, streak, and preferred theme. The questionnaire result is based on your answers; it is not a medical measurement.

Network activity and sharing

The verified Android release manifest does not request Internet access. DryCare's current code does not upload your profile, questionnaire answers, or progress to the developer or share them with advertisers or data brokers. It includes the Google Fonts library for typography, but the Android release cannot fetch fonts over the Internet without Internet permission. No account, analytics, advertising SDK, or developer backend is configured.

Google Play, Android, and your device provider may handle installation or diagnostic information under their own policies. Such platform activity is separate from the records DryCare stores in its local database.

Security

DryCare stores its records in app-local storage, which Android isolates from ordinary access by other apps. Android backup is disabled for DryCare. The Hive database is not separately encrypted, so device security, including your screen lock, remains important. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Your entries stay in app-local storage until you erase them using Settings → Reset All Data or uninstall DryCare. Reset All Data clears the local profile, questionnaire, results, daily records, streak, and preferences. DryCare has no account or developer-held copy to delete. The app cannot erase copies you may have made outside its storage, such as device screenshots.

Health information

DryCare provides general skincare and wellness information. Its questionnaire and suggestions do not diagnose a condition, assess clinical severity, or replace medical advice or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for persistent or worsening dryness, painful cracks, bleeding, signs of infection, or other concerning symptoms. Follow any personal medical advice about fluid intake.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its “Last updated” date if DryCare's data practices change. Review the current policy here when you update the app.
